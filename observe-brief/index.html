Instrument 08 / Observe

Check the premise before you send.

The working form of the first lesson. One account. Ten minutes. One decision you can defend: send, verify, or stop.

Start the brief Return to the lesson Download the worksheet

Keep confidential evidence in your own secure workspace. This form does not upload your answers. A hypothesis is not a receipt.

The operating receipt

01 / Name the observation

Write what changed, without explaining why yet.

02 / Attach the evidence

Save the source and its date. Check whether it still describes the current situation.

03 / Separate the hypothesis

Write “I think this matters because…” and name what would prove that wrong.

04 / Choose the next action

Identify one owner, one recipient, and a specific question or useful contribution.

05 / Set a stop condition

If the source is stale, the premise is wrong, or the person is not responsible, research again instead of sending.

After the response

Record what changed. No response is not proof that the hypothesis was right.

Decision

Send, verify, or stop.
