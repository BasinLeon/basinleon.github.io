Executive Pressure-Test

Dragon Questions for GTM Builders.

Click any question from the workshop Q&A to inspect the operator answer.

Why do most AI outbound stacks fail within 60 days?

They automate volume instead of judgment. Sending 10x more generic AI emails destroys domain reputation and buyer trust. Agents should own research, CRM hygiene, and first-pass briefs — never unreviewed external sends.

How did you cut SDR technical ramp from 90 days to 5?

Replaced passive slide decks with just-in-time certification and automated account briefs. Reps did not memorize 200 features before dialing; the system handed them verified buyer context and exact technical proof at the moment of outreach.

What is the difference between a CRM and Flow::Nexus?

A CRM is a database of past history. Flow::Nexus is the decision layer above it: capturing a public signal, checking the premise, verifying ownership, enforcing a human gate, and logging whether the call actually moved pipeline.

How do you measure a GTM system without lying to yourself?

Measure stage velocity, not call volume. Apply a 7-day kill-or-promote rule to stalled deals so zombie opportunities do not inflate forecasts, and verify live CRM state rather than trusting automated activity counts.
