03 · The rules, in evaluation order

The first matching rule wins. Technical and sales keyword counts determine the category; ties are Mixed. The original Python UI calls this category “Company DNA.”

  1. P03+ sales/GTM matches, Technical category, and strength ≥ 60.
    Applied rule
  2. P12+ sales/GTM matches and Technical category.
    Applied rule
  3. P22+ sales/GTM matches, or Technical category.
    Applied rule
  4. P3All remaining inputs.
    Applied rule

For Technical or Sales, strength is the winning keyword count × 20, capped at 100. Mixed uses a fixed 50. These formula values reproduce the prototype’s rules; they are not calibrated confidence estimates.

What the rules can tell you.

They expose which words caused a tier. Keyword matches need human verification: a mention of a sales role does not confirm hiring, budget, authority, or purchase intent. Technical words do not verify someone’s background.
