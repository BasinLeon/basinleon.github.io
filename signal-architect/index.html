How the logic works.

Three steps, exactly as the tool runs them. The scan is a plain substring match over the full row text (company, contact, title, and notes combined, lowercased). It matches letters, not intent, and each keyword counts once per row no matter how often it appears. That rawness is deliberate: the logic is the filter, and a human still reviews the queue it produces.

Step 1

Friction Filter

Counts how many of these hiring keywords appear in the row: sales, account executive, ae (the letters ae followed by a space, catching AE as a standalone title), sdr, bdr, business development, revenue. One point per keyword found. A company hiring for sales roles is showing intent to build sales infrastructure, and that friction is the buying signal.

Step 2

DNA Analysis

Counts two keyword families. Technical: engineer, cto, vp engineering, developer, technical founder, phd, security, infosec. Sales: vp sales, cro, chief revenue, sales leader, head of sales. More technical matches means Technical DNA, more sales matches means Sales DNA, and an even count means Mixed DNA. Confidence is the winning count times 20, capped at 100; a tie is always 50.

Step 3

Priority

P0 when friction is 3 or more, the DNA is Technical, and confidence is 60 or more. P1 when friction is 2 or more and the DNA is Technical. P2 when friction is 2 or more, or the DNA is Technical. Everything else is P3. The queue is worked top down: P0 first, P3 only when the top of the list is clear.
