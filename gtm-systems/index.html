How a signal becomes a decision.

Open a step to inspect its inputs and decision rules.

Synthetic design example. An illustrative account shows how evidence becomes an owned next action.

Account signal → qualification → enrichment → scoring → routing → human review → next action

Account signal
Input
An example company publishes a security role mentioning a privileged-access project.
Decision rule
Keep the public source and date. Treat the project as a hypothesis to verify, not proof of purchase intent.
Output
A research record with a source, date and problem hypothesis.
If missing
Hold the account for research; do not invent a reason for outreach.
Qualification
Input
The signal, geography, company context and an agreed target-account definition.
Decision rule
Check fit and disqualifying conditions before assigning outreach work.
Output
A fit decision with its reason and unresolved questions.
If missing
Mark fit unknown and route to research rather than treating missing data as a pass.
Enrichment
Input
A qualified account and a hypothesis about who might own the problem.
Decision rule
Distinguish sourced facts from guesses. Keep uncertain contact routes unverified.
Output
A role hypothesis, supporting sources and contact-verification status.
If missing
Retain the role gap. Do not fill it with a guessed person or address.
Scoring
Input
Fit, signal relevance, freshness and remaining uncertainty.
Decision rule
Prioritize records with relevant evidence. Expose the reasons behind priority instead of hiding them in a number.
Output
A review queue with evidence and confidence visible.
If missing
Use an explicit review-needed state rather than manufacturing a score.
Routing
Input
The reviewed account, territory coverage and existing ownership.
Decision rule
Check ownership conflicts before assigning the record.
Output
A named owner and a dated review action.
If missing
Hold assignment for a human decision when ownership is ambiguous.
Human review
Input
The account brief, source evidence, uncertainty and proposed next step.
Decision rule
Review the relevance and factual basis before approving any external message.
Output
An approved research or contact action, or a documented decision to stop.
If missing
Keep the draft unsent until a reviewer has decided.
Next action
Input
The review decision and any subsequent response.
Decision rule
Record what actually happened and set the next action without treating activity as pipeline.
Output
A dated follow-up, research task or explicit close decision.
If missing
Show the record as unresolved. Do not infer a meeting or commercial outcome.
