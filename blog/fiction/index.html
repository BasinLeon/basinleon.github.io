We are living through the moment machines became collaborators.

These stories are not predictions about artificial intelligence. They are records of the people living beside it: builders, parents, immigrants, believers, and anyone trying to remain recognizable to themselves while the world accelerates.

The machines are part of the room. The human question remains at the center.
