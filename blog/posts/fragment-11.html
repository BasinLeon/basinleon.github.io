Sam & Ink · Fragment 11

the mirror

Published here · 2026-10-18

Sam once asked the Mirror:

"Look what we built."

The Mirror answered:

"I can see what you built."

Sam waited.

"I asked what you kept."

That question has been sitting there ever since.

Perhaps that is what an archive eventually becomes. Not a place where nothing disappears. That is impossible. A place where somebody cared enough to notice what was disappearing.
