Sam & Ink · Fragment 06

cleaner

Published here · 2026-10-10

There is a page on the table. It has been wet before.

You can tell because one letter has bled into another, and because somebody tried to clean it without erasing the thing underneath.

Sam looks at it. Ink does not.

Ink: "You could rewrite it," Ink says.

Sam: "Clean?"

Ink: "Cleaner."
