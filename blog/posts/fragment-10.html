Sam & Ink · Fragment 10

the pause

Published here · 2026-10-14

In the heart of North Beach, my play begins. A sidewalk café, the clatter of a typewriter, the scent of espresso

Ink looks again at the wet page.

"Should I save it?"

"No."

Ink waits.

"Write down where I found it."

And for once, neither of them tries to improve the sentence.
