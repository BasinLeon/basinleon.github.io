Sam & Ink · Fragment 07

the window

Published here · 2026-10-10

Outside the window, Silicon Valley continues doing what Silicon Valley does.

Lights in office buildings. Cars moving along highways. Servers remembering things nobody will ever ask for again. A number moving somewhere on a dashboard. Somebody calling it progress.

None of it enters the room except as light.
