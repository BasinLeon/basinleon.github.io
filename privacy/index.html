Reader privacy

The work can be measured without following you around.

This site uses privacy-minded analytics to understand which pages are read, which links are useful, how readers arrive, and whether the experience works across devices.

What can be measured

Pageviews, link clicks, referrer domains, campaign tags, scroll depth, active reading time, device class, and coarse geography may be recorded. These signals help improve the archive and its navigation.

What is not stored

No advertising profile is built. No cross-site tracking cookie is used. Literal IP addresses are not written to the private event dataset. When first-party event collection is enabled, the edge replaces the request IP with an irreversible keyed token before storage.

Your controls

The shared measurement client honors Global Privacy Control and browser Do Not Track. The hidden “After hours” studio clock is a separate, owner-oriented instrument. Its visit and reading counters remain only in that browser.

Retention and contact

First-party event data is designed for a 90-day retention window. Questions can be sent to lbasin23@gmail.com.
