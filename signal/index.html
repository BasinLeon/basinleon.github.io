The operating chain

Signal → Build → Proof → Writing → Conversation.

Finished products are the residue. This page shows the chain that produces them — what is noticed, what is built from it, what the evidence says, what gets written, and who talks back.

  1. 01

    Signal

    Something noticed in the field, before it is an argument. Small, specific, unresolved.

  2. 02

    Build

    The smallest useful answer — a model, a diagram, a system someone can act on.

  3. 03

    Proof

    Evidence that it worked, or honest evidence that it didn’t. Both move the work forward.

  4. 04

    Writing

    The idea made legible — Notes, anchor essays, fiction, longer work.

  5. 05

    Conversation

    A reader, buyer, or builder talks back. Did somebody encounter the work and then start a conversation? That is the KPI.
