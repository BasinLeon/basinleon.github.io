## What to review after distribution

Record which link was shared, where, and when. Review source/campaign visits,
returning readers, and private form submissions in Site Traffic. The first
tagged source is preserved across internal pages in the same tab for 30 minutes,
where analytics is permitted. Owner exclusion, automated-browser exclusion,
DNT, and GPC still prevent analytics initialization.

A form receipt is an inquiry, not a qualified opportunity. An email click is
intent, not proof of delivery. Record actual replies and next actions privately
in the execution ledger. Do not infer revenue from page views.

Contact forms currently save to the private Website conversations inbox in
Site Traffic. They do not send an email notification. Check that inbox when
reviewing distribution results.
